# Style preview assets

The Styles gallery contains illustrative website examples, not claims of completed client projects. Two existing assets are reused unchanged: `public/images/business-website-demo.png` and `public/images/online-store-demo.png`.

The following new assets were created with the built-in image-generation tool and are included in the project. Their prompts are retained here for future revisions.

## public/images/styles/warm-minimal.png

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-end, clean, modern interior design website EXAMPLE image for a web designer's style gallery. Landscape 3:2 canvas. Present a realistic straight-on slim dark laptop occupying most of the frame, and a matching slim phone at the lower right, on a uniform near-black navy background #080c13. The whole devices are visible with modest margins, no external captions. Screen design: warm ivory background, dark brown editorial serif headlines mixed with crisp sans-serif navigation, generous whitespace, precise grid. Fictional studio wordmark 'STUDIO FORMA'. Desktop screen shows a refined split hero: left text exact 'Spaces to feel at home.' and a small 'Explore our work' button; right beautiful photorealistic neutral living room, natural stone, walnut and linen sofa, warm sunlight. Below hero a neat row of two large project photo thumbnails and small tasteful project labels. The mobile screen shows the same refined cream layout with stacked image and headline. Make the interface polished and plausible, all major type cleanly spelled, photos beautiful, no invented awards, testimonials, stats, badges, watermarks, designer credits, or sales claims. Photorealistic screen/device presentation, clear luminous readable screen, no extreme perspective. This is a website design illustration, not a real client case study.

## public/images/styles/fresh-natural.png

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-end, clean, modern landscaping website EXAMPLE image for a web designer's style gallery. Landscape 3:2 canvas. Present a realistic straight-on slim dark laptop occupying most of the frame, and a matching slim phone at the lower right, on a uniform near-black navy background #080c13. The whole devices are visible with modest margins, no external captions. Screen design: fresh off-white background, sophisticated forest green accents, very clean contemporary sans-serif typography, generous whitespace, precisely aligned navigation and content. Fictional company wordmark 'FIELDWORK'. Desktop screen shows a large pale-cream typographic hero top left with exact heading 'Room to grow.' and small 'Explore our gardens' button. Beside it a beautiful photorealistic landscaped garden with pale stone path, natural planting, mature green trees, contemporary warm cedar privacy fence and tranquil outdoor sitting area. A thin row of simple service labels below: 'Design', 'Build', 'Care', then two neat large project images partially visible near bottom. The mobile screen uses the same palette and stacks the hero headline and garden image. Make it an excellent professional website, restrained and contemporary. Clean correct major lettering. No invented reviews, awards, numbers, social proof, badges, watermark, designer credits. Photorealistic screen/device presentation, crisp screens and realistic lighting, no extreme perspective. This is a website design illustration, not a real client case study.
